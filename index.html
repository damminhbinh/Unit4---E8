STILT HOUSES

Traditional Architecture and Heritage of Vietnamese Ethnic Minorities

[A] Stilt houses are popular among different ethnic minority groups, from the Thai in the Northern Highlands to the Khmer in the Mekong Delta. The houses come in different sizes and styles, and show the traditional culture of their owners.

[B] Stilt houses are made from natural materials like wood, bamboo, and leaves. They stand on strong posts, about two or three metres above the ground. This allows them to keep people safe from wild animals. People climb a seven- or nine-step staircase to enter the house. The most important place in the house is the kitchen. It has an open fire in the middle of the house. It is the place for family gatherings and receiving guests.

[C] The stilt houses of the Tay and Nung usually overlook a field. The stilt houses of the Thai, however, face mountains or a forest. The Bahnar and Ede have a communal house (called a Rong house) as the heart of their village. These communal houses are the largest and tallest ones in the village.

2. Reading Comprehension: Tick True (T) or False (F)
1. Only a few minority groups live in stilt houses.
🎯 Dẫn chứng: Đoạn [A]: "Stilt houses are popular among different ethnic minority groups, from the Thai in the Northern Highlands to the Khmer in the Mekong Delta."
💡 Giải thích: Bài đọc khẳng định nhà sàn rất phổ biến ở "nhiều nhóm dân tộc thiểu số khác nhau" (different ethnic minority groups), do đó nhận định "chỉ một vài nhóm" (only a few) là Sai => False.
2. All stilt houses look alike.
🎯 Dẫn chứng: Đoạn [A]: "The houses come in different sizes and styles, and show the traditional culture of their owners."
💡 Giải thích: Các ngôi nhà sàn có nhiều kích cỡ và kiểu dáng phong cách khác nhau (different sizes and styles), chứ không hề giống nhau hoàn toàn (look alike) => False.
3. Family gatherings take place by the open fire in the middle of the house.
🎯 Dẫn chứng: Đoạn [B]: "It has an open fire in the middle of the house. It is the place for family gatherings and receiving guests."
💡 Giải thích: Bếp lửa ở giữa nhà chính là nơi sinh hoạt, tụ họp gia đình và đón tiếp khách khứa => True.
4. The Rong house serves as the centre of an Ede village.
🎯 Dẫn chứng: Đoạn [C]: "The Bahnar and Ede have a communal house (called a Rong house) as the heart of their village."
💡 Giải thích: Nhà rông đóng vai trò như trái tim / trung tâm (the heart = the centre) của buôn làng người Ba Na và Ê Đê => True.
